A NOTE ON THE MODEL

A vision of your home.

This interactive interpretation follows the typical floor plan and exterior illustration in the Arjith Anugraha brochure.

Apartment areas and room dimensions are taken from the brochure. Wall positions are traced from the drawing. Ceiling heights, finishes, furnishings, landscape, parking and unseen elevations are illustrative.

Five residential floors above a ground-level entrance are inferred from the 15-home brief and three flats per typical floor. The same typical plan is used for every floor. This is a spatial preview, not a construction or approval drawing.

Flat A’s duplicate bedroom labels have been renamed for easier navigation. No claim is made about carpet area, structural accuracy or final finishes.
