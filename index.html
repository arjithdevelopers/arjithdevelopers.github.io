Built by an engineer, for families

Arjith Property Developers was founded in 1999 by Er. V. Gouthaman, a civil engineer from Anna University and a qualified structural consultant. That engineering background is the company — homes are designed for structural soundness first, then dressed beautifully.

Working alongside architect S. Subramanian (S.S. Associates), the firm has grown into one of Trichy's respected builders, known for clear title and an honest way of doing business.

“We build the home we would want our own family to live in.” — Er. V. Gouthaman, Founder
